# Blue Prince -- Builds and strategies

**status:** research-integrated
**last_reconciled:** 2026-10-31

There are no loadouts in the usual sense; "builds" are drafting habits plus which permanent upgrades and items you chase. Advice here is community opinion, not rules; where it is one player's view it says so. Source numbers in brackets are listed in `## Sources`. Design note: where a plan here repeats an achievement goal, `achievements.md` owns the trigger, `mechanics.md` owns the rule, and this file holds only the player-facing plan; the repetition is intentional and each copy keeps its own tier.

## Where guides converge

> **Cross-system dependency** -- see `dependencies.md` DEP-023.

- Fill the lower ranks sideways before going north and do not leave unopened doors behind you; Ranks 1-2 never have locked doors and Rank 3 almost never.
- Keep two routes north and use spare slots for dead ends so they stop being offered later; do not over-draft exits that point to the same square.
- Take notes: every guide and thread read says a notebook is essential (the game has no journal).

Draft rooms you have never seen before, and visit the Drafting Studio each time it appears (each visit adds a permanent room).

## Where they disagree

Dead ends: one Reddit post says "don't be afraid of dead ends, if you avoid them early it's all you're going to draft later", against the common fear of dead ends. This is a style disagreement, not a fact conflict.

Single-opinion tips (forum, unverified): do not draft a Passageway in the first five ranks; do not fear the Chapel (three doors are more important than coinage).

## Play styles (this report's synthesis; no source names them)

Steps-focused: Bedrooms and food, the Sleep Mask (+5 steps on the first Bedroom, per TrueAchievements), the Sauna and Master Bedroom next-day bonuses, the Apple Orchard (+20, raising the day to 70 steps); Reddit also suggests turning the Gymnasium lights off at the Utility Closet breaker so the room does not cost steps. Resource-focused: gems first ("prioritize gems early", "don't pass up the Wine Cellar"), the Lucky Rabbit's Foot, Patio and Locker Room spreads, keys as the likely cause of a dead run. Hybrid: the Utility Closet and Garage first, Boiler Room power on first so powered doors draft power-compatible rooms, Mail Room packages and Commissary special orders.

## Plans for achievements that point at earlier rooms

> **Cross-system dependency** -- see `dependencies.md` DEP-029.

Full House plan: start in the bottom-right corners, remove dead ends, and let the Sauna's extra steps help. Trophy of Wealth plan: you need at least 300 gold in the run.

## Blessings (Shrine, an Outer Room)

> **Cross-system dependency** -- see `dependencies.md` DEP-011.
> **Cross-system dependency** -- see `dependencies.md` DEP-014.
> **Cross-system dependency** -- see `dependencies.md` DEP-015.

Offering 1-80 coins at the Shrine gives one blessing at a time; removing your gold from the bowl ends it and curses you. Blessings: Berry Picker (a berry instead of a floorplan gives a random plan ignoring rarity), Chef, Dancer (pay 1 gem to spin floorplans), Gardener (adds 8 Courtyards), General (5 gems after a Red Room with Red Rooms in 5 ranks), High Roller (ivory die per Shop; 5 extra coins per roll), Monk (call it a day in a house room to draft that room from the grounds next day), Tinkerer (a Mechanical room triggers your experiment). Coin ranges for 3 / 4 / 5 / 6 / 7 days are on the wiki page (for example Dancer 1-2 coins buys 3 days; Monk 13-14). Patch 1.7.3 swapped the three-day Tinkerer and Chef gold amounts "to correctly align their intended blessing thresholds".
_source: P1 research 2026-10-31 refs [1][61] · capture: web_fetch · confidence: medium · enemy-tier: 1 · puzzle-tier: 1 · category: mainline · spoiler: progression_ [Single source -- verify · class:community-wiki]

Reddit (2025) says "one coin lets you pay a gem to rotate rooms, three coins a reroll die every time you make a shop, five coins tons of greenhouses"; those figures differ from the wiki page's three-day ranges (which start at 1-2, 3-4 and 5-6 coins) and may reflect the 2025-04 build.
_source: P1 research 2026-10-31 refs [52][61] · capture: web_fetch · confidence: low · enemy-tier: 1 · puzzle-tier: 1 · category: mainline · spoiler: progression_ [Contradicted across sources -- see notes]

P2 (TrueTrophies page 13) gives the cost of each blessing for 3, 4, 5, 6 and 7 days. Day costs as listed: Dancer 1 / 17 / 33 / 49 / 65 (pay 1 gem to spin floorplans); High Roller 3 / 19 / 35 / 51 / 67; Gardener 5 / 21 / 37 / 53 / 69 (adds 8 Courtyards to the draft pool); Chef 7 / 27 / 43 / 59 / 75; Red General 9 / 25 / 41 / 57 / 73; Tinkerer 11 / 23 / 39 / 55 / 71; Monk 13 / 29 / 45 / 61 / 77; Berry Picker 15 / 31 / 47 / 63 / 79. The three-day costs for the Dancer (1), High Roller (3) and Gardener (5) equal the figures the 2025 Reddit post quotes above and sit at the low end of the wiki ranges (for example Dancer 1-2, Monk 13-14), so the apparent Reddit-versus-wiki mismatch may be a reading of ranges against single costs. This is the guide's own comparison, not a source statement. The 2025 Reddit post was not re-read in P2, and the page does not say whether its Chef and Tinkerer three-day amounts (7 and 11) come before or after patch 1.7.3 swapped them, so the conflict stays open.
