# Blue Prince -- Upgrades (permanent additions)

**status:** research-integrated
**last_reconciled:** 2026-11-01

Permanent additions carry over between runs. Six estate upgrades sit on the Grounds, plus Drafting Studio additions, Upgrade Disks, rarity changes and other carry-overs. Combinations and solutions the wiki pages do not state are marked as gaps. Source numbers in brackets are listed in `## Sources`.

## Estate upgrades

### West Gate Path

> **Cross-system dependency** -- see `dependencies.md` DEP-021.

Effect: Outer Room access each day. Unlock: in a Utility Closet switch on the Garage Power breaker; press the Garage door button (the Garage only appears in the West Wing); walk the West Path; lift the bar on the West Gate; it stays open for good. Side effect: the Utility Closet loses its early draft boost afterwards. A Rustic Door outside then offers one extra draft per day.
_source: P1 research 2026-10-31 refs [29][36] · capture: web_fetch · confidence: medium · enemy-tier: 1 · puzzle-tier: 1 · category: mainline · spoiler: progression · missable: no_ [Single source -- verify · class:community-wiki]

P2: TrueTrophies says the gate must be opened from the other side by leaving through a powered Garage (the Utility Closet powers the Garage); TrueAchievements says the West Gate Path "provides a path from the Entrance Hall to the Outer Room without the need for the Garage" and calls it free. The two are consistent only if the Garage route is the first-time method, which neither page says. From day 2 the Utility Closet is Commonplace until the West Gate is opened.
_source: P2 research 2026-10-31 refs [1][5][13][19] · capture: browser_fetch · confidence: low · enemy-tier: 1 · puzzle-tier: 1 · category: mainline · spoiler: progression_ [Contradicted across sources -- see notes]

### Apple Orchard

> **Cross-system dependency** -- see `dependencies.md` DEP-006.
> **Cross-system dependency** -- see `dependencies.md` DEP-008.
> **Cross-system dependency** -- see `dependencies.md` DEP-015.

Effect: +20 steps each day from the next day (70 steps in total). Unlock: the Orchard Gate south of the campsite has a four-wheel number lock; the right combination slides the bar open. Extension from guides: leave the manor and walk down the driveway; the code itself is in `puzzles/safes_and_codes.md`; a Reddit post says the clue is a date on a tree in a picture of the orchard, readable with the Magnifying Glass, and that the route involves drafting the Darkroom and turning the power on at the Utility Closet breaker afterwards (entering the Darkroom trips the power). Where the picture sits: [unlock-chain incomplete -- container/tier unknown].

The gate's code (held in `puzzles/safes_and_codes.md`) came from one walkthrough in P1 and is now also given by P2's TrueTrophies page 13 and a Russian VGTimes page; the Darkroom and breaker detail still comes from one forum post only.

### Gemstone Cavern

> **Cross-system dependency** -- see `dependencies.md` DEP-008.
> **Cross-system dependency** -- see `dependencies.md` DEP-022.

Effect: +2 gems each day. Unlock: solve the Utility Closet fuse box (six colored lights that change when pressed; the correct six colors raise a hidden giant switch); pull it; the campsite cave door opens. The wiki does not give the correct colors or say which Utility Closet; P2's TrueTrophies puzzle page does give the steps (kept at the late-game tier in `puzzles/utility_closet_vac.md`) but also says only "this room" for the closet. Reddit calls it the "V.A.C." puzzle on the Utility Closet breaker, needing the computer password (readable with the Magnifying Glass in rooms such as Security, and inferable from the car's license plate).
_source: P1 research 2026-10-31 refs [31][36][52][56] · capture: web_fetch · confidence: high · enemy-tier: 1 · puzzle-tier: 1 · category: mainline · spoiler: progression_ [Confirmed: 3 sources, 1 languages]

The computer-password link to the cavern comes from one forum post only.

### Blackbridge Grotto

> **Cross-system dependency** -- see `dependencies.md` DEP-003.
> **Cross-system dependency** -- see `dependencies.md` DEP-004.
> **Cross-system dependency** -- see `dependencies.md` DEP-005.
> **Cross-system dependency** -- see `dependencies.md` DEP-025.

Effect: access one offline terminal from the network each day. Unlock: the Laboratory machine must be powered by Boiler Room steam piped through ducts; solve its switch puzzle; after a lockdown and countdown the Grotto is powered and unlocked for good. The wiki does not give the switch puzzle steps. TrueTrophies says to pull lever 9 then 3 on the Laboratory machine once the Boiler Room is powered; Reddit says the Laboratory must be powered from the Boiler Room and a "periodic table puzzle" explains how to flip the switches. The Boiler Room power route is now described in `puzzles/machinery.md` (P2); whether it must be redone each day is still not stated [unlock-chain incomplete -- container/tier unknown: per-day power route].

### Satellite Dish

> **Cross-system dependency** -- see `dependencies.md` DEP-005.
> **Cross-system dependency** -- see `dependencies.md` DEP-006.

Effect: upload experimental data packets to the Laboratory; it stops blocking inbound email and offsite communications. Unlock: open the Apple Orchard first; open the sundial's lid at the back of the Orchard to a microchip pedestal; insert all three microchips; the dish rises on the campsite. The wiki does not say where the microchips are; guides do: one is at the Blackbridge Grotto pedestal, one is in the vase in the Entrance Hall (break it with a Sledge Hammer or Power Hammer; a Reddit reply says an ordinary sledge hammer works), one is buried at a dig spot by the west bridge to the Outer Room; chips stay found permanently but must be carried to use. At the sundial, TrueTrophies says to burn the sundial base twice with the Burning Glass and line up the cracks; Reddit says use the Burning Glass for scorch marks and line up the base.
_source: P1 research 2026-10-31 refs [33][27][53][56] · capture: web_fetch · confidence: high · enemy-tier: 2 · puzzle-tier: 2 · category: mainline · spoiler: late-game_ [Confirmed: 3 sources, 1 languages]

### Blue Tents

> **Cross-system dependency** -- see `dependencies.md` DEP-012.

Effect: each Blue Room drafted on the 8th Rank contains a hidden Blue Memo. Unlock: have 8 trophies in the Trophy Room; buy at the Mount Holly Gift Shop for 400 coins (the Gift Shop joins the pool after the first arrival at the goal room); the tents are added to the campsite.
_source: P1 research 2026-10-31 refs [34][36][48] · capture: web_fetch · confidence: medium · enemy-tier: 2 · puzzle-tier: 2 · category: mainline · spoiler: late-game_ [Single source -- verify · class:community-wiki]

An early 2025 Spanish article (3DJuegos) listed "up to four" permanent upgrades available almost from the start (West Gate, Gemstone Cavern, Apple Orchard, Blackbridge Grotto), rating Gemstone Cavern and Apple Orchard easy, West Gate normal and Blackbridge Grotto hard; the English wikis now list six because the Satellite Dish and Blue Tents were added later (a date difference, not a contradiction). How an Upgrade Disk works: at a terminal each disk picks one of 15 rooms at random and offers 3 upgrades to choose from; extra disks re-offer upgrades of an already upgraded room, and for the Spare Room extra disks offer only the final three. A terminal exists in the Office, Security, the Laboratory and the Shelter, and all terminals accept disks.
_source: P2 research 2026-10-31 refs [4][9] · capture: browser_fetch · confidence: medium · enemy-tier: 1 · puzzle-tier: 1 · category: mainline · spoiler: progression_ [Single source -- verify · class:editorial-en]

Early disk sites (16 are listed in all; one is a trade route and one a repeatable purchase, and the page orders them easiest to hardest): the Office desk; a one-time 15-gold purchase at the Commissary; the Morning Room (needs Bacon & Eggs eaten, via the Breakfast Nook upgrade or the Kitchen); the Great Hall behind a locked corner door; Her Ladyship's Chamber counter (draft south along the west wing); the Garage car trunk (Car Keys, on the first opening); the Trading Post wall (dynamite plus the Burning Glass); the Archives centre cabinet (File Cabinet key dug up in front of the Patio BBQ); and a Trading Post trade (Master Keys or Emerald Bracelets). Extra disks can be bought with Master Keys at the Trading Post.
_source: P2 research 2026-10-31 refs [4] · capture: browser_fetch · confidence: medium · enemy-tier: 1 · puzzle-tier: 1 · category: mainline · spoiler: progression_ [Single source -- verify · class:editorial-en]

Deeper disk sites (late-game): the Freezer behind an iced doorway (Furnace, Burning Glass, Torch or Power Hammer); the bottom of the Foundation elevator; the Reservoir candle-room stone; the Tomb entrance candles; the Mechanarium (draft it after enough mechanical rooms so that the fifth door opens); Lost and Found (dug up in the basement; it spawns among the first rare items); and a Vault box (four vault keys, randomly found; leave them in the Coat Check).
