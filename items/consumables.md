# Blue Prince -- Consumables

**status:** research-integrated
**last_reconciled:** 2026-11-01

Food restores steps; a few other items are used up. Source numbers in brackets are listed in `## Sources`.

## Food

Food adds steps and nothing else: apple 2, banana 3, orange 5, cupcake 10. The wiki's Items page lists Food among its Resource Items.
_source: P1 research 2026-10-31 refs [3][5][24] · capture: web_fetch · confidence: high · enemy-tier: 0 · puzzle-tier: 0 · category: mainline · spoiler: none_ [Confirmed: 3 sources, 1 languages]

Kitchen dishes (a Shop room): Banana 2 coins and Club Sandwich 8 coins are always stocked; the third dish rotates (Bacon & Eggs 8, Chef Salad or Tomato Soup 5).

## Used-up items

> **Cross-system dependency** -- see `dependencies.md` DEP-024.
> **Cross-system dependency** -- see `dependencies.md` DEP-034.

- Keys, coins and gems are spent as you use them and reset each day (see `mechanics.md`).
- Repellent removes the room you stand in from the draft pool for a week.

_source: P1 research 2026-10-31 refs [3][24][26][35][55] · capture: web_fetch · confidence: medium · enemy-tier: 0 · puzzle-tier: 0 · category: mainline · spoiler: none_ [Single source -- verify · class:community-wiki]

- A Wind-Up Key is consumed when you open a box in the three-box puzzle (see `puzzles/parlor_three_boxes.md`).
- A Master Key is not consumed and is lost at day's end; a Keycard is not consumed.
- The Furnace and Sauna can unfreeze coins and gems frozen in the Freezer; see the missable note in `sections/missables.md`.
