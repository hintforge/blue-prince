The Armory items are tools, not weapons: the Morning Star breaks a cursed object, The Axe makes a room's gem cost free for later days (up to 3 rooms), the Torch lights candles, and the Armory sells them only while you hold a particular chess power (Morning Star, Torch and Knight's Shield at 8 coins and The Axe at 32).
_source: P1 research 2026-10-31 refs [26][35][37][48] · capture: web_fetch · confidence: high · enemy-tier: 2 · puzzle-tier: 2 · category: mainline · spoiler: late-game_ [Confirmed: 4 sources, 1 languages]

Special Keys group (wiki.gg): Basement Key, Car Keys, Key 8, Keycard, Master Key, Prism Key, Sanctum Key, Secret Garden Key, Silver Key. Do not merge them with the minor keys that open ordinary locked doors. Several names sit in two wiki groups: the Master Key under both Special Keys and Showroom Items; the Basement Key, Key 8 and Sanctum Key under both Special Keys and Unique Items; the Allowance Token under both Resource Items and Unique Items.

## Lock Pick

Whether a Lock Pick gives one attempt per lock or allows retries is disputed: one wiki says one attempt; a Reddit tips post says you can retry and it may work on the second or third try, while its replies split (one never succeeded after a first failure, another opened rooms on a second attempt with a lock-related contraption, others doubt retries).
_source: P1 research 2026-10-31 refs [24][51] · capture: web_fetch · confidence: low · enemy-tier: 0 · puzzle-tier: 0 · category: mainline · spoiler: none_ [Contradicted across sources -- see notes]

P2 did not settle it: TrueAchievements describes the Lockpick Kit only as a "chance of being able to open a door without the need for a key", and neither P2 page says whether a lock allows one attempt or retries.
_source: P2 research 2026-10-31 refs [12][13] · capture: web_fetch · confidence: low · enemy-tier: 0 · puzzle-tier: 0 · category: mainline · spoiler: none_ [Contradicted across sources -- see notes]

A late laboratory effect named "permanently increase lockpicking skill" also exists (P2, TrueAchievements); it is held at the late-game tier because it comes from the satellite-era experiments (`puzzles/grotto_and_satellite.md`).
_source: P2 research 2026-10-31 refs [13] · capture: web_fetch · confidence: medium · enemy-tier: 2 · puzzle-tier: 2 · category: mainline · spoiler: late-game_ [Single source -- verify · class:editorial-en]

The Workshop combines inventory items:

| Contraption | Made from | tier |
|---|---|---|
| Burning Glass | Magnifying Glass + Metal Detector | progression |
| Powered Electromagnet | Compass + Battery Pack | progression |
| Detector Shovel | Shovel + Metal Detector | progression |
| Dowsing Rod | Shovel + Compass | progression |
| Jack Hammer | Shovel + Battery Pack + Broken Lever | progression |
| Lucky Purse | Coin Purse + Lucky Rabbit's Foot | progression |
| Pick Sound Amplifier | Lock Pick Kit + Metal Detector | progression |
| Power Hammer | Sledge Hammer + Battery Pack + Broken Lever | progression |

_source: P1 research 2026-10-31 refs [41][42] · capture: web_fetch · confidence: high · enemy-tier: 1 · puzzle-tier: 1 · category: mainline · spoiler: progression_ [Confirmed: 2 sources, 1 languages]

P2's TrueAchievements walkthrough (page 4, truncated after three recipes) repeats the Burning Glass, Power Hammer and Jack Hammer recipes, so those three have a third publication; the five recipes it did not reach are all in the table above from P1, so P2's "remaining 5 unknown" gap is closed by P1. The Power Hammer (crafted at the Workshop) is a different object from the plain Sledge Hammer base item.
_source: P1 research 2026-10-31 refs [41][42] and P2 research 2026-10-31 refs [13] · capture: web_fetch · confidence: high · enemy-tier: 1 · puzzle-tier: 1 · category: mainline · spoiler: progression_ [Confirmed: 3 sources, 1 languages]

What the Burning Glass is used for (late-game): candles and fuses in the Chapel, the Freezer, the Abandoned Mine, the Tomb, the Trading Post dynamite and the Tunnel torches. What the Power Hammer breaks (late-game): weak walls in the Greenhouse, the Freezer, the Secret Garden, the Weight Room, the Basement crate-maze and the Precipice chess-room.
_source: P2 research 2026-10-31 refs [13] · capture: web_fetch · confidence: medium · enemy-tier: 2 · puzzle-tier: 2 · category: mainline · spoiler: late-game_ [Single source -- verify · class:editorial-en]

Build all eight contraptions to earn a trophy: set the Workshop's rarity to common via the Conservatory (or the Gear Wrench) to see it more; duplicates do not count; the trophy spawns on the Workshop table. Not missable.

## Shops

> **Cross-system dependency** -- see `dependencies.md` DEP-010.
> **Cross-system dependency** -- see `dependencies.md` DEP-012.
> **Cross-system dependency** -- see `dependencies.md` DEP-013.
> **Cross-system dependency** -- see `dependencies.md` DEP-014.
> **Cross-system dependency** -- see `dependencies.md` DEP-027.
> **Cross-system dependency** -- see `dependencies.md` DEP-029.

The Commissary sells four items from one of seven stock sets (Gems 3, Shovel 6, Key 10, Sledge Hammer 8). The Locksmith sells 1 Key for 5, 3 Keys for 12, a Special Key for 8 (random Silver, Secret Garden or Prism) and a Lock Pick for 10. The Showroom sells four of six luxury items priced 20-80 coins. The Laundry Room swaps one resource for another 1:1 for 5-10 coins. The Bookshop sells six books for 8-50 coins, once each. The Casino allows one roulette wager per day (5, 20 or 100) and slot pulls for 1 coin. The Trading Post trades items and takes no coins. Digging needs a Shovel, sold at the Commissary and found in rooms such as the Toolshed.
_source: P1 research 2026-10-31 refs [24][48] · capture: web_fetch · confidence: medium · enemy-tier: 1 · puzzle-tier: 1 · category: mainline · spoiler: progression_ [Single source -- verify · class:community-wiki]

Buy out the entire Showroom (all four shown items) and then the final Trophy of Wealth item for 100 coins; you need about 300 coins in a run (the shop page lists items at 20-80 coins). Not missable.
_source: P1 research 2026-10-31 refs [25][41][42][43][48] · capture: web_fetch · confidence: high · enemy-tier: 1 · puzzle-tier: 1 · category: mainline · spoiler: progression · achievement: trophy_of_wealth · achievement-hidden: no · trigger_type: mastery · missable: no_ [Confirmed: 5 sources, 1 languages]

P2 (TrueAchievements walkthrough page 5): the Showroom (rare) offers 4 of 6 items: Moon Pendant 30, Silver Spoon 30, Chronograph 30, Ornate Compass 50, Emerald Bracelet 60, Master Key 80. After four purchases the Trophy of Wealth item costs 100 gold; the run needs 240 gold (120 with a constellation buff) up to 320. The Sail Sale constellation (half price) first appears at 18 stars, then at 23, 27, 30, 32, 35, 38, 42, 43, 46, 47 and 48. P1's shop page lists items at 20-80 coins, so the lowest price differs between the two.
_source: P2 research 2026-10-31 refs [14] · capture: web_fetch · confidence: medium · enemy-tier: 1 · puzzle-tier: 1 · category: mainline · spoiler: progression · achievement: trophy_of_wealth · achievement-hidden: no · trigger_type: mastery · missable: no_ [Single source -- verify · class:editorial-en]

The Gift Shop is a secret room that joins the draft pool after the first arrival at the goal room; souvenirs cost 10-400 coins, and the Swim Bird Plushie (110 coins) unlocks a challenge mode.
_source: P1 research 2026-10-31 refs [28][37][41][42][48] · capture: web_fetch · confidence: high · enemy-tier: 2 · puzzle-tier: 2 · category: mainline · spoiler: late-game_ [Confirmed: 3 sources, 1 languages]

P2 (TrueAchievements, three pages: one site): the Mount Holly Gift Shop becomes available once Room 46 has been reached; it sells the Swim Bird Plushie (110), the Cursed Coffer (13) and the Blue Tents (400 gold and at least 8 trophies), and it must be built for the Explorer's Trophy.
_source: P2 research 2026-10-31 refs [13][14][15] · capture: web_fetch · confidence: medium · enemy-tier: 2 · puzzle-tier: 2 · category: mainline · spoiler: late-game · achievement: explorers_trophy · achievement-hidden: no · trigger_type: collection_ [Single source -- verify · class:editorial-en]
