## Rules of the exam

> **Cross-system dependency** -- see `dependencies.md` DEP-018.

The exam is taken in the ninth Classroom after nine Classrooms are built in one go (the Schoolhouse, drafted as the Outer Room, adds the other eight Classroom plans; a Classroom costs 1 gem and is L-shaped). It runs on a real-time 30-minute timer. Once an answer is chosen it cannot be changed, and the answers never change between attempts. An A (45 of 46) or A+ (46 of 46) is needed; the result and the trophy arrive next day in the Entrance Hall envelope or on the Entrance table.
