## Tomb angels and candle walls

> **Cross-system dependency** -- see `dependencies.md` DEP-026.

Raise the seven angels' arms in order I to VII; a wrong angel restarts the sequence, and the way below is entered through the Tomb's angel wall. The Tomb's candle walls need a Torch or another fire item.
_source: P1 research 2026-10-31 refs [35][36] · capture: web_fetch · confidence: medium · enemy-tier: 2 · puzzle-tier: 2 · category: mainline · spoiler: late-game_ [Single source -- verify · class:community-wiki]

P2 names the seven angel statues by what each holds, in order: Hoe, Pizza Spatula, Rake, Chimney Brush, Broom, Horse Whip, then the Scepter, which opens the path to the Catacombs; the Scythe opens the exit to the south underground. The seven count agrees with P1.
_source: P2 research 2026-10-31 refs [5] · capture: browser_fetch · confidence: medium · enemy-tier: 2 · puzzle-tier: 2 · category: mainline · spoiler: late-game_ [Single source -- verify · class:editorial-en]

What the candle walls hold is reported three ways. P1: the first wall drops two Ivory Dice and an Upgrade Disk for the Cloister. TrueTrophies (P2): the wall by the entrance holds an Upgrade Disk and 6 dice the first time (2 dice afterwards), and a second candle wall holds a Diary Key and a Mora Jai box. TrueAchievements (P2): the first candle wall gives an Allowance Token and the Diary Key. Which wall holds the Diary Key, the Mora Jai box and the Allowance Token is unresolved.

## Antechamber levers

> **Cross-system dependency** -- see `dependencies.md` DEP-004.
> **Cross-system dependency** -- see `dependencies.md` DEP-033.

The Antechamber's four sealed walls are opened by levers elsewhere: north by an Inner Sanctum lever (or a Throne Room backup); east by a Great Hall wall lever or the Secret Garden's east lever; south by the Greenhouse lever after repair with a Broken Lever; west by the Secret Garden's west lever or behind door 5 of the Mechanarium. One opened wall leads to the door marked 46. The P2 routes for each lever, the Throne Room source, the Antechamber doors and the lever-count disagreement are in `puzzles/antechamber_levers.md`.

## Blackbridge terminals

Use at least four of the five terminals before the admin keys work; "MoorR" and "ThomK" are admin and "BabbA" is personnel. The admin function imports trophies between saves, which merges the challenge-mode and fastest-run saves into the main save. The derivation of these passwords names characters from the story (first four letters of a surname plus an initial); TrueTrophies gives "RibbJ" instead (three TrueTrophies pages in P2 repeat it, which is one site). TrueAchievements (P2) gives "MoorR" for the Administrator Terminal, so "MoorR" now appears in P1's wiki or Reddit source (the line does not say which) and in TrueAchievements, and "RibbJ" only on TrueTrophies; the P2 result's guess that the two belong to different terminal states or game versions is unverified. The menu path differs too: "import data settings" (TrueTrophies) against "Admin Options" then "Import Trophies" (TrueAchievements). Several keys are listed, including delisted ones, so they are not necessarily exclusive.

## Underground chess puzzle

> **Cross-system dependency** -- see `dependencies.md` DEP-008.
> **Cross-system dependency** -- see `dependencies.md` DEP-009.

Turn four gas valves (in the Apple Orchard, Schoolhouse, Hovel and Gemstone Cavern) to reach it. Within one day, draft at least one room holding each chess piece (color and duplicates do not matter) and match the manor layout to the board; the king and queen rooms are the hard part. The reward is one permanent chess power per solve (re-solve to switch); the Knight power is the only way to see the Armory. P2's account of the valves, the elevator, the six powers and the Scepter path is in `puzzles/chess_puzzle.md`.
_source: P1 research 2026-10-31 refs [48][52][53][56] · capture: web_fetch · confidence: high · enemy-tier: 2 · puzzle-tier: 2 · category: mainline · spoiler: late-game_ [Confirmed: 4 sources, 1 languages]

A castle move (set the Rook or King power one day and the other the next day) opens a further room.

## Key of Aries clock puzzle

Lvl 1 (nudge): the chamber past the chess puzzle matters at one specific in-game time.
_source: P1 research 2026-10-31 refs [53] and P2 research 2026-10-31 refs [5] · capture: web_fetch · confidence: high · enemy-tier: 2 · puzzle-tier: 2 · category: mainline · spoiler: late-game_ [Confirmed: 2 sources, 1 languages]

Lvl 2 (more): you need an empty inventory and a particular constellation active.
_source: P1 research 2026-10-31 refs [53] and P2 research 2026-10-31 refs [5] · capture: web_fetch · confidence: high · enemy-tier: 2 · puzzle-tier: 2 · category: mainline · spoiler: late-game_ [Confirmed: 2 sources, 1 languages]

Lvl 3 (answer): with the Observatory and the Southern Cross constellation active and no items, gems, keys or gold in inventory, at 1:30 pm in-game a clock-tomb rises in the crypt chamber past the chess puzzle. Set 0109 and press; add six minutes to 0115 and press; rewind until the minute hand rests on EAST (1226) and press. This gives the Key of Aries and the raised tomb stays open. P2's TrueTrophies page gives the same three settings (1:09, 1:15, then "EAST"); the star counts it lists for the step are in `puzzles/chess_puzzle.md`.

## Vault box and blank books

Convert MCCXIII to 53, which also sums to 8; use the Room 8 key at vault box 53; the resulting phrase is "STILL WATER TINTS BLANK BOOKS" (distilled water, from the Trading Post fuse and the distillery, applied with the Watering Can to blank books in the Library). With the Monk blessing the Secret Passage becomes an Outer Room where still water pulls a blue book, and the Atelier follows.
