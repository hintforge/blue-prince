## Method added by P2 (late-game)

Source number [9] is P2's TrueTrophies page 13 (Puzzle Solutions). The maths runs from the centre outward: the bullseye-to-triple space, the triple, the triple-to-double space, then the double. The colours give the operation: Blue adds, Yellow subtracts, Pink multiplies, Purple divides. The modifiers are: a square in the bullseye; a diamond (reverse the digits); a one-third-filled mark (divide by 3); 2, 4 or 6 wavy lines (round to 1, 10 or 100); an X (ignore); a slash (halve); two dots (double). The red X, slash and two dots agree with P1's chalk-mark description.
