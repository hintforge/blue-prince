## What it is and where the clues are

> **Cross-system dependency** -- see `dependencies.md` DEP-021.
> **Cross-system dependency** -- see `dependencies.md` DEP-022.

Six V.A.C. indicator lights in a Utility Closet change colour when clicked. Solving them raises a hidden lever; the same closet also holds the other breaker-style switches (Gymnasium negative effects off, Darkroom lights, Garage power). The page does not describe a "fuse-box colour" puzzle inside the Gemstone Cavern itself, and it says only "this room" without naming which Utility Closet; P1's wording (a fuse box of six coloured lights that unlocks the cavern) is therefore left unresolved: the result does not say whether the two descriptions are the same puzzle.

## Hint ladder

Lvl 1 (nudge): the six lights change colour when clicked, and the order you click them in matters; learn what one click does before trying to solve all six.
_source: P2 research 2026-10-31 refs [9] · capture: derived · confidence: low · enemy-tier: 1 · puzzle-tier: 1 · category: mainline · spoiler: progression_

Lvl 3 (solution), from the page: 1) set all six lights green (this unlocks blue); 2) click the first green to blue and keep clicking blues until the first five are blue and the sixth is grey; 3) click the grey until it is red, then click blue number five so the red turns purple; 4) repeat red-then-click-the-right-blue until the first five are purple, then click the sixth grey to purple; 5) click light 4 (it turns white); 6) click light 5 four times to red, then light 6 once (blue) so that light 5 turns purple; 7) click light 6 twice (red) and light 5 twice (red); 8) click light 3 three times to green; 9) click light 1 twice to grey. The wall opens to reveal a lever, which unlocks the Gemstone Cavern and Garage power.
