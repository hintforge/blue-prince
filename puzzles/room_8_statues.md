## What it is

Room 8 is a puzzle room reached through a locked door on a Rank 8 room, opened with a numbered key from the Gallery puzzle (that key only works entering Rank 8). It holds eight animal statues, each tied to a sin, each with a rhyme about its placement. This is not the goal room (see "Terms that look alike" in `mechanics.md`).

## Hint ladder

Lvl 1 (nudge): each statue has a sin rhyme; read each rhyme for where its statue stands.

Lvl 2 (more): the sin-animal pairs are lion envy, penguin gluttony, bunny avarice, dog mundanity, elephant wrath, swan lust, bear sloth, monkey hubris. P2's TrueTrophies page 13 [9] (https://i2.truetrophies.com/game/Blue-Prince/walkthrough/13; [14] is TrueAchievements walkthrough page 5) lists the same eight pairs (its statue bins: hubris monkey, envy lion, lust swan, wrath elephant, gluttony penguin, sloth bear, avarice rabbit, mundanity dog).
