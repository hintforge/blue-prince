## What it is

Seven red envelopes are marked 1, 2, 4, 5, six, VII and 8 (none is marked 3). They are found in the Watch Tower (underground; a four-wheel lock), the Study, the Boudoir, the Drafting Studio, the Drawing Room, the Bomb Shelter and the Office.
