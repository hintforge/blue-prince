## What it is

> **Cross-system dependency** -- see `dependencies.md` DEP-024.

The Parlor (also drafted as a Funeral Parlor) holds a three-box logic puzzle. The Puzzles page lists 25 puzzles in six groups; its answers are fixed except for this puzzle, which is a fresh instance each box (the page's worked example is "puzzle 1").

## Hint ladder

Lvl 1 (nudge): only one box has the prize; read what each box claims.
_source: P1 research 2026-10-31 refs [35] · capture: web_fetch · confidence: medium · enemy-tier: 0 · puzzle-tier: 0 · category: mainline · spoiler: none_ [Single source -- verify · class:community-wiki]

Lvl 2 (more): at least one box's statements are entirely true and at least one entirely false.
_source: P1 research 2026-10-31 refs [35] · capture: web_fetch · confidence: medium · enemy-tier: 1 · puzzle-tier: 1 · category: mainline · spoiler: progression_ [Single source -- verify · class:community-wiki]

Lvl 3 (step by step): test each box as the prize box against those three rules. Opening a box uses up a Wind-Up Key, so you get one guess.
_source: P1 research 2026-10-31 refs [35] · capture: web_fetch · confidence: medium · enemy-tier: 1 · puzzle-tier: 1 · category: mainline · spoiler: progression_ [Single source -- verify · class:community-wiki]

Lvl 3 (worked example): the page's worked example gives the Blue box.
_source: P1 research 2026-10-31 refs [35] · capture: web_fetch · confidence: medium · enemy-tier: 2 · puzzle-tier: 2 · category: mainline · spoiler: late-game_ [Single source -- verify · class:community-wiki]

P2's TrueTrophies (pages 11 and 13, references [4] and [9]) words the rule as three boxes and one Wind-up Key, where one box lies, one tells the truth and one is "free"; P1's wiki says at least one box's statements are entirely true and at least one entirely false. The two framings are not reconciled. A Parlor with the two-Wind-up-Key upgrade gives about a 66 percent chance when guessing blind.

## Reward link

Winning 40 three-box games earns an achievement; each box opening consumes a Wind-Up Key.
