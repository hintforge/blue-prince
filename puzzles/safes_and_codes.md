## What it is

Several rooms hold a safe with a fixed combination, and some terminals and doors take passwords. A memo in the Mail Room says the digits 6, 7 and 9 never appear in a safe's fixed combination. Patch 1.4.5 made safes stay open on later days once unlocked, and patches mention regional alternate answers (the Boudoir safe accepts one).

## Safes, codes and passwords added by P2 (late-game)

Source numbers in brackets are the P2 research result's numbering, listed in `## Sources (P2)`. Locations of the hidden safes: a corner of the Boudoir; the Drawing Room (pull the left candle on the fireplace); the Office (a switch in the desk drawer); beside the Study door; the Drafting Studio (a tiny safe on the table, needs the Magnifying Glass, costs no gem); the Orchard gate (no gem or red letter). Each of the first four safes holds a gem (free gems).
_source: P2 research 2026-10-31 refs [9][22] · capture: browser_fetch · confidence: medium · enemy-tier: 2 · puzzle-tier: 2 · category: mainline · spoiler: late-game_ [Single source -- verify · class:editorial-en]

Each of the first four safes also holds a red letter (lore).
_source: P2 research 2026-10-31 refs [9] · capture: browser_fetch · confidence: medium · enemy-tier: 3 · puzzle-tier: 3 · category: lore · spoiler: story_ [Single source -- verify · class:editorial-en]

The codes as TrueTrophies gives them: Boudoir 1225; Drawing Room 0415; Office 0303; Study 1208; Drafting Studio 1108 (no gem); Orchard gate 1128; the underground gate behind the Red Door (also the second-floor gate in the Mechanarium chain) MAY8. The Shelter's time-lock safe has no code: set the unlock time at least 1 hour ahead on the same day (Day 1 is November 7; each day starts at 8am).
_source: P2 research 2026-10-31 refs [5][9] · capture: browser_fetch · confidence: medium · enemy-tier: 2 · puzzle-tier: 2 · category: mainline · spoiler: late-game_ [Single source -- verify · class:editorial-en]

VGTimes (Russian) agrees on the Orchard gate code, the Office code, the Drafting Studio code (a calendar read with the Magnifying Glass; the second day, 8 November), the Drawing Room code (left candlestick over the fireplace; the count of men and women in the paintings) and the Red Door code. It gives the Boudoir code as 2512 or 1225 (the digit order can differ), and gives the Study code as 0812 (the queen on D8 of the chess board read as 8 December) where TrueTrophies gives 1208. P1's sources already list 0812 and 2512 as accepted alternates for the Study and Boudoir, so the two pages may be listing different accepted orders rather than disagreeing; that is not verified. [translated from: Russian]

Case may be typography rather than a difference; P1 prints a longer footage password too.
