## Opening the Grotto

> **Cross-system dependency** -- see `dependencies.md` DEP-003.
> **Cross-system dependency** -- see `dependencies.md` DEP-004.
> **Cross-system dependency** -- see `dependencies.md` DEP-005.

The Grotto opens when the Laboratory is powered through the Boiler Room and the numbered levers 9 and 3 are flipped. The Boiler Room and the Laboratory must be built next to each other for power to reach a device in the Laboratory; the numbered-lever clue is in the periodic table in the Laboratory, and a cutscene shows the way outside. Inside are an Administrator Terminal (right of the front door) and a central pedestal with 1 Microchip and 2 empty sockets; chips stay on the pedestal unless moved (an earlier game version reset them each day). The pedestal leads to the Throne Room floorplan.

Throne Room: with all 3 microchips in the central base a door opens up the mountain to the floorplan. The Throne Room costs 5 gems and is not a Blue room; it is a north Antechamber lever source and holds one of the eight Sanctum keys.

## Microchips

> **Cross-system dependency** -- see `dependencies.md` DEP-005.

Three microchips exist, one pre-placed on the Grotto pedestal. One is in a vase in the Entrance Hall that is broken with a sledgehammer; one is dug up at a hidden spot by the bridge to the Outer Room, beside the West Gate. A route with a sledgehammer and a shovel is mentioned for the third but not located. TrueAchievements hides the locations behind click-to-reveal blocks and says the "long way" starts at the Bookshop (rare) buying particular books. How many chips can be carried at once is not stated.

## Satellite Dish

> **Cross-system dependency** -- see `dependencies.md` DEP-005.
> **Cross-system dependency** -- see `dependencies.md` DEP-006.
> **Cross-system dependency** -- see `dependencies.md` DEP-020.

The Satellite Dish needs the Burning Glass and all three microchips: scorch the inner and centre rings of the Sundial stand, turn both rings so all lines align (the outer ring is fixed), then the top flips open; insert the chips (a cutscene plays). Take the chips back to the Grotto pedestal. After the Administrator Terminal login, the Laboratory's "Packet Management" and "Create Custom Packet" let the player pick 8 triggers and 8 effects (Advanced Experiments). Recommended effects: remove a crate from the tunnel (run for several days), unseal one of the Antechamber doors (per day only), permanently increase lockpicking skill, lower the Reservoir by 1.

The satellite leads to a second ending, which is not an achievement.

## Terminal functions

> **Cross-system dependency** -- see `dependencies.md` DEP-016.
> **Cross-system dependency** -- see `dependencies.md` DEP-025.

The room terminals and their functions (all accept Upgrade Disks; the Laboratory terminal's daily experiment) are in `mechanics.md` section "Terminals". With the satellite, the Laboratory terminal can download more experiments and build a list of 8 triggers and 8 rewards. Grotto terminal: import trophy data from other saves ("import data settings" per TrueTrophies; "Admin Options" then "Import Trophies" per TrueAchievements). An "Admin Key" login used in the satellite era has its answer behind a click-to-reveal block that could not be read.

The Grotto's Administrator Terminal also removes the dead-room hazard for Upgrade Disks (a disk is lost if no Office can be drafted to reach a terminal).
