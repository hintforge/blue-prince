## What it is

> **Cross-system dependency** -- see `dependencies.md` DEP-030.

Mora Jai boxes sit in several rooms (the early ones: Master Bedroom, Solarium and Trading Post; the rest are under "Later content"). You press tiles until all four corners show the box's target color; each box is solved once. The tile rules per color: yellow moves north, violet moves south, green swaps with the opposite tile, red turns black to red and white to black, orange takes the majority color of its neighbors, pink rotates its neighbors, black shifts its row right. Each solved box gives an Allowance Token (+2 allowance).

## Added by P2

P2's TrueTrophies page 14 [24] adds three rules to the colour list: white turns this and nearby white tiles grey and nearby grey tiles white; blue copies the effect of the centre tile; grey does nothing. It gives pink as rotating adjacent squares clockwise and says the box opens when all four corners hold the right colour and the outer corner buttons are pressed; a wrong corner colour resets the box. The page's solutions are images and could not be read.

That is 7 named sites plus 8 Sanctum rooms (15 boxes) against the 8 boxes P1 listed; the P2 inventory is incomplete, and which Tomb wall holds the box is disputed (see `puzzles/secret_puzzles.md`).
