## The four levers

> **Cross-system dependency** -- see `dependencies.md` DEP-004.

To open Room 46 the north Antechamber lever must be thrown. Four levers (north, west, east, south) are named, each with several routes. A Polish guide title (not opened) says three levers and the basement key; the P1 summary and this result say four.
_source: P2 research 2026-10-31 refs [1][5] · capture: browser_fetch · confidence: low · enemy-tier: 2 · puzzle-tier: 2 · category: mainline · spoiler: late-game_ [Contradicted across sources -- see notes]

North lever: the Inner Sanctum underground holds the north lever; the most consistent Day One route is the "Underground Lever", needing both the south and the north sides of the Reservoir. Alternates: the Throne Room floorplan (advice for Curse Mode) and an experiment reward "Open an Antechamber lever". The Throne Room does not work as a lever source on Day 1 because it is added to the next day's draft.
_source: P2 research 2026-10-31 refs [1][3][5] · capture: browser_fetch · confidence: medium · enemy-tier: 2 · puzzle-tier: 2 · category: mainline · spoiler: late-game_ [Single source -- verify · class:editorial-en]

West lever: the Secret Garden (its key is needed first), or the Mechanarium with several mechanical rooms drafted. At the Secret Garden's weathervane, moving two arrows to face the third reveals the west lever.
_source: P2 research 2026-10-31 refs [1][9] · capture: browser_fetch · confidence: medium · enemy-tier: 2 · puzzle-tier: 2 · category: mainline · spoiler: late-game_ [Single source -- verify · class:editorial-en]

East lever: the Great Hall (always in one of the four corner doors; a silver key on the centre three columns helps find the room), or the Secret Garden with a Power Hammer: break the nearby wall to reach a third valve and set all of them to the opposite direction. With the Garage drafted north of the garden and powered, the east lever can also be reached from outside the back wall.
_source: P2 research 2026-10-31 refs [1][9] · capture: browser_fetch · confidence: medium · enemy-tier: 2 · puzzle-tier: 2 · category: mainline · spoiler: late-game_ [Single source -- verify · class:editorial-en]

South lever: the Greenhouse (the broken lever is needed to repair it; it can only be drafted going south in the West Wing or north in the East Wing), or the Weight Room (behind a wall broken with the Power Hammer; costs about half the steps).

## Doors and floorplans tied to the Antechamber

> **Cross-system dependency** -- see `dependencies.md` DEP-011.

The Orinda Cloister upgrade opens up to three Antechamber doors; only four Blackprints can be drafted (Treasure Vault, Armory, Aquarium, and the non-blue Throne Room, which costs 5 gems).
_source: P2 research 2026-10-31 refs [4] · capture: browser_fetch · confidence: medium · enemy-tier: 2 · puzzle-tier: 2 · category: mainline · spoiler: late-game_ [Single source -- verify · class:editorial-en]

Naming note: the "Treasure Trove" (an Outer Room floorplan and key-trunk room tied to the chess puzzle and the Royal Scepter) and the "Treasure Vault" (a Blackprint drafted for the Antechamber doors) are different rooms.
_source: P2 research 2026-10-31 refs [4][5] · capture: browser_fetch · confidence: medium · enemy-tier: 2 · puzzle-tier: 2 · category: mainline · spoiler: late-game_ [Single source -- verify · class:editorial-en]

The Mechanarium is the floorplan with eight doors (also a key site); "gear room" or "gear puzzle" is the underground site where its floorplan is found, solved by spinning a gear to align paths and pressing raise. The same chain lists a powered red box in the Boiler Room that opens the Red Door, a second-floor gate with its own code (see `puzzles/safes_and_codes.md`), and the Treasure Trove with a gem and a red letter inside.

## Foundation elevator

A hidden crank lowers the Foundation's elevator: draft a room with an exit facing the one Foundation side that has none, then interact with the gears in the doorway. Below, the Basement Key from the Antechamber is needed to leave the room; the elevator leads to the underground and to an Upgrade Disk.
