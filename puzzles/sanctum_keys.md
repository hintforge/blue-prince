Eight keys open the eight doors of the Inner Sanctum; each use removes the key permanently. Reaching the goal room is the prerequisite. The sites as one guide lists them:
1. The goal room's desk, only after the credits.
2. Vault box 370, one of four Vault keys (leave the key in the Coat Check until the Vault is drafted).
3. The Clock Tower floorplan from the Drafting Studio: wait for 1:30 in-game, go through the hidden door, set the clocks.
4. The Reservoir at 0 units: a lone black box with a moon, behind the rock pillar, opened with a regular key.
5. The Throne Room, reached by Laboratory power, levers 9 then 3 and three microchips.
6. A hidden station reached by the Reservoir levels 10 then 6.
7. The Music Room, drafted on Rank 9 after at least 37 rooms have been drafted.
8. The Mechanarium: eight doors each opened by a gear-icon room, or the diagonal-door trick with four mechanical rooms.

## Reservoir and pumps

> **Cross-system dependency** -- see `dependencies.md` DEP-020.

The Reservoir holds at most 11 units of water. At 10 the boat works between its north and south sides; at 6 the player walks down the north steps and takes the boat to a hidden station (a key site); at 0 the basin is empty and shows trunks, and a white box with a gold sun holds 3 Allowance tokens. Lowering the water: the Pump Room with power adds 4 units to its reserve tank, or a satellite experiment permanently removes 1 unit. The underground boat must be left on the north side, so a second way in and out (the elevator or an opened Tomb) is needed.

## Sigils and the Trophy of Sigils

The eight realm sigils, described left to right as one guide lists them:
1. Y-shaped, 3 lines with 3 lines on the ends, rectangles with a corner missing, alternating small and large circles.
2. 4 lines in an X with 3 lines on the ends, fangs, an unconnected wavy line.
3. Y-shaped lightning-bolt lines, squares with a dot, VVVVVVVV.
4. Y-shaped 3 parallel lines, flowers, alternating horizontal and vertical lines.
5. 4 lines in an X with a chain of circles, 3 lines, alternating up and down triangles.
6. 8 single lines, a cell with 3 dots, circles.
7. 5 wavy lines with one going north-east, pizzas, a connected line of -u-'s.
8. 5 bendy lines only on top, fangs, diagonal lines.
_source: P2 research 2026-10-31 refs [2] · capture: browser_fetch · confidence: medium · enemy-tier: 2 · puzzle-tier: 2 · category: mainline · spoiler: late-game_ [Single source -- verify · class:editorial-en]

After all eight keys are found, the player inputs the right sigil in each of the eight Inner Sanctum rooms; a Mora Jai box appears in each room afterwards, and the sigils do nothing otherwise. The trophy is found on the Entrance table; P1 says it spawns in the Inner Sanctum foyer, and the two placements are unreconciled.
