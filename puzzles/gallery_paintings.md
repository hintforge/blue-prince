## What it is

> **Cross-system dependency** -- see `dependencies.md` DEP-001.

The Gallery holds a set of paintings whose titles are words of 5, 6, 7 and 8 letters. Patch 1.4.5 made the 7-letter title accept an overlooked regional spelling.

## Hint ladder

Lvl 3 (step by step): the titles are THINK, PONDER, REALIZE, RUMINATE. Two open paintings open the right case (2 gems); four open the left case (a numbered key that a later puzzle needs). P2's TrueTrophies lists the same four words.

## Painting clues added by P2 (late-game)

P2 source numbers: [9] https://i2.truetrophies.com/game/Blue-Prince/walkthrough/13 ; [14] https://www.trueachievements.com/game/Blue-Prince/walkthrough/5 ; [19] https://blueprince.wiki.fextralife.com/Progression.

P2 pairs each painting's picture with its word: the THICk painting is "Think", a large P is "Ponder", eyeballs are "Realize" and an infinity-style 8 is "Ruminate". Opening the showcases gives the Room 8 key; a door on Rank 8 then lets Room 8 be drafted. The Gallery is not offered before day 46 on a fresh save (Fextralife) while TrueAchievements calls it "a rare spawn, generally at higher Ranks"; the two partly agree.
