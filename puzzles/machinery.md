## Boiler Room steam

> **Cross-system dependency** -- see `dependencies.md` DEP-003.

Powering the Boiler Room matters for powered doors and, on a longer chain, for the Laboratory (see `items/upgrades.md`; the day-by-day power route is [unlock-chain incomplete]).
_source: P1 research 2026-10-31 refs [35] · capture: web_fetch · confidence: medium · enemy-tier: 1 · puzzle-tier: 1 · category: mainline · spoiler: progression_ [Single source -- verify · class:community-wiki]

Central pipe: open Boiler A, turn Pipe Switcher One once, set Pipe Switcher Two to position 3. Left: open Boiler B and leave Lever B. Right: open Boiler C and flip Lever C. Then press ACTIVATE.

Boiler Room power, as one guide words it (late-game): turn on boiler 1; rotate the cross pipe toward the next pipes; connect to boiler 2 (metal plates move); stop the steam while the plates do not block boiler 2; switch it on; turn on boiler 3 (upper floor, behind a gate); flip the small switch on boiler 3 toward the big one; ride the elevator down; point boiler 2's switch up; route boiler 1's steam up to the retractable bridge and the generator; then use the generator's centre console on the upper floor. P1's central, left and right branch settings above are a different description of the same room and are not reconciled with this one.
_source: P2 research 2026-10-31 refs [9] · capture: browser_fetch · confidence: medium · enemy-tier: 2 · puzzle-tier: 2 · category: mainline · spoiler: late-game_ [Single source -- verify · class:editorial-en]

Pump Room (late-game): the six tanks are the Fountain, the Reservoir, the Aquarium, the Kitchen, the Greenhouse and the Pool; the reserve tank can only be drained to when the Pump Room is powered. Fountain empty: the basement is entered from outside (the Basement Key is needed). Aquarium empty: hints to the filing-cabinet key. Kitchen full: the Watering Can can be refilled. Greenhouse full: a Watering Can always spawns and gems grow as flowers. Pool empty: coins at the bottom, or two locked trunks with Swim Trunks. Draining the Fountain with the Pool and then the Pump Room leads to the basement door underground.
_source: P2 research 2026-10-31 refs [5][9] · capture: browser_fetch · confidence: medium · enemy-tier: 2 · puzzle-tier: 2 · category: mainline · spoiler: late-game_ [Single source -- verify · class:editorial-en]
