## Reaching the chess room

> **Cross-system dependency** -- see `dependencies.md` DEP-008.
> **Cross-system dependency** -- see `dependencies.md` DEP-018.

Four gas valves sit in the Apple Orchard, the Gemstone Cavern, the Schoolhouse and the Hovel (the last two are Outer Rooms). With all four open the blue-flame elevator runs to the cliffside underground; it is used from outside only and cannot be called down from below. The chess room is the room on the right at the bottom.
_source: P2 research 2026-10-31 refs [3][5][6] · capture: browser_fetch · confidence: high · enemy-tier: 2 · puzzle-tier: 2 · category: mainline · spoiler: late-game_ [Confirmed: 2 sources, 2 languages]

A Polish guide describes the four blue flames as lit on a stone belvedere east of the main entrance, and says the elevator then rises from the depths. [translated from: Polish]

Naming note: a TrueTrophies underground map calls the area reached from the outside elevator or the candle ring the "cliffside passage" or "cliffside entrance"; the Polish guide's names for the cliffside area are "Urwisko" and "Precypicjum". Guides also say "Precipice" for the same place.

## The puzzle and its powers

Within one day, draft one room for each chess piece and match each to its position on a 9 by 5 board that mirrors the manor. The per-piece room lists are on the TrueTrophies page (reference [5]) and are not reproduced here; the Polish page lists further rooms in images that could not be read. The Polish guide adds that the solution changes with each day's house layout.
_source: P2 research 2026-10-31 refs [5][6] · capture: browser_fetch · confidence: high · enemy-tier: 2 · puzzle-tier: 2 · category: mainline · spoiler: late-game_ [Confirmed: 2 sources, 2 languages]

Each solve lets the player choose one permanent power; the chosen power stays until the puzzle is solved again and a different one is picked.
_source: P2 research 2026-10-31 refs [5][6] · capture: browser_fetch · confidence: high · enemy-tier: 2 · puzzle-tier: 2 · category: mainline · spoiler: late-game_ [Confirmed: 2 sources, 2 languages]

| Piece | Power | Effect | tier |
|---|---|---|---|
| Pawn | Ambition of the Pawn | At Rank 8, choose a Queen, Rook, Bishop or Knight power | late-game |
| Knight | Mantle of the Knight | Adds the Armory to the draft pool | late-game |
| Bishop | Piety of the Bishop | No Chapel tithe; +30 coins on the first Chapel entry each day | late-game |
| Rook | Resilience of the Rook | 4 rerolls in each of the 4 corner drafts | late-game |
| Queen | Gambit of the Queen | A West Wing draft costs 5 steps and gives 1 key | late-game |
| King | Banner of the King | Choose a colour each day; that colour is more common | late-game |

## Armory

> **Cross-system dependency** -- see `dependencies.md` DEP-009.
> **Cross-system dependency** -- see `dependencies.md` DEP-010.
> **Cross-system dependency** -- see `dependencies.md` DEP-026.

With the Knight power the Armory is drafted; it sells a Torch and the Axe (32 coins; removes the gem cost of a floorplan; 3 permanent uses). The Polish guide says the Axe reduces the cost of gem rooms. The Armory is also named as a requirement of the Explorer's Trophy.

## Royal Scepter path

> **Cross-system dependency** -- see `dependencies.md` DEP-011.

One TrueTrophies row says the Scepter path runs through the Rook power and then the King power; another row of the same result says to choose the Knight power, then the King power, then the Rook power (which triggers a cutscene and opens a passage). P1 describes a "castle move" (the Rook or King power set one day and the other the next day). The order is unresolved.
_source: P2 research 2026-10-31 refs [5] · capture: browser_fetch · confidence: low · enemy-tier: 2 · puzzle-tier: 2 · category: mainline · spoiler: late-game_ [Contradicted across sources -- see notes]

The next step is run at 1:30 in-game with an empty inventory and the Southern Cross constellation active; the result lists twelve star counts at which this works (9, 13, 16, 25, 29, 32, 33, 36, 40, 45, 46, 49). The Observatory table elsewhere in the same result gives the Southern Cross as a 5-star constellation, which does not match any of those counts; the result does not explain the difference.
_source: P2 research 2026-10-31 refs [5][9] · capture: browser_fetch · confidence: low · enemy-tier: 2 · puzzle-tier: 2 · category: mainline · spoiler: late-game_ [Single source -- verify · class:editorial-en]

Set the rotary clock to 1:09, turn it clockwise to 1:15, then counter-clockwise to "EAST"; the Key of Aries appears. P1's Reddit source gives the same three settings (0109, 0115, and 1226 as EAST).
_source: P2 research 2026-10-31 refs [5] · capture: browser_fetch · confidence: high · enemy-tier: 2 · puzzle-tier: 2 · category: mainline · spoiler: late-game_ [Confirmed: 2 sources, 1 languages]

Next, the Blessing of the Monk (13, 29, 45, 61 or 77 coins at the Shrine, by days) is taken, and the day is ended inside the Treasure Trove so that it is offered as an Outer Room; opening its trunk gives the Royal Scepter. A separate row says the Treasure Trove, with a gem and a red letter inside, sits behind a second-floor gate in the Mechanarium chain; the two access routes are not reconciled.
