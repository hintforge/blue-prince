## What it is

> **Cross-system dependency** -- see `dependencies.md` DEP-017.

The tower room (a Drafting Studio addition) holds eight clocks and eight notes. Setting all eight clocks right gives a Sanctum Key from the drawer of the fifth grandfather clock.
