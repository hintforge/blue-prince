**status:** research-integrated

Room inventory from the wiki.gg Room Directory [2] and the Fextralife Rooms, Room Types and Shops pages [46][47][48] (all P1, 2026-10-31). Treat the Directory as a starting point: it lists 8 rooms per colored group, while Fextralife counts more because of multicolor and added rooms. Rarity is draw frequency, not rank. Each row hides or shows on its own `tier`. Rooms the research did not describe beyond a name are listed by name only; per-room effects for all 110 rooms are a gap.

Totals: the Directory counts about 110 rooms (46 numbered rooms, 40 colored and 24 others per Fextralife); the "not listed" variants of some rooms (for example Funeral Parlor) appear to be upgraded or special versions of base rooms (hypothesis, not researched).

TrueAchievements counts about 90 plans at the start plus 18 to collect (up to 110), with 3 counted as already built (the Entrance Hall, the Antechamber and one more). wiki.gg calls the Outer Room "Shelter" and Fextralife calls it "Bomb Shelter" (the same room).
_source: P1 research 2026-10-31 refs [2][46][55] · capture: web_fetch · confidence: medium · enemy-tier: 1 · puzzle-tier: 1 · category: mainline · spoiler: progression_ [Contradicted across sources -- see notes]

P2 additions (TrueTrophies and TrueAchievements, one publisher network): the Outer Room offers 8 possible rooms, and 2 dice guarantee seeing the Tomb among them; the Trophy Room is a blue room and rarer. The wiki.gg room template read by P2 lists the same Studio additions, found floorplans and eight Outer Rooms (Toolshed, Shelter, Schoolhouse, Shrine, Root Cellar, Hovel, Trading Post, Tomb) as P1.
